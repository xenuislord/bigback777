# A week with HEARTH

## Get around with fewer steps

Use the search button in the top bar, or **⌘/Ctrl-K**, to find a saved recipe by ingredient or jump to a page. Arrow keys choose a result; Enter opens it and Escape closes it. Page and selected week are kept in the address, so browser Back/Forward and reload return to the same view.

Recipe filters, sorting, and selected recipes stay in this browser tab while you visit other screens. Clear selections has an Undo action. Adding them to the calendar clears that draft. Signing out clears these device-only preferences.

The home screen shows the next useful step for the selected week: choose recipes, review a draft, or open groceries. **Plan next week** selects the upcoming week before opening the recipe collection.

## Recipes and preferences

The recipe collection starts with 60 complete original recipes, including 20 dinners and eight each of breakfasts, lunches, snacks, desserts and drinks. All have ingredients, quantities, original three-person portions, full steps and storage instructions. Chilling/freezing time is stated in the steps separately from active preparation.

Search names, ingredients and tags. Category, cooking time, preferred protein, estimated per-serving cost, and favorite filters work together. **Find ideas** ranks saved recipes by the craving words and returns twice your requested count (at least 12 when possible). Strict filters can produce fewer; nothing is invented to fill the grid.

A heart is an explicit favorite. “Not for us” explicitly hides a recipe; merely scrolling past it does not. Hidden recipes can be restored in Settings. Enjoyment ratings and notes persist. Personal photo uploads stay in the household database, with a 1 MB upload limit. Starter illustrations are original drawings, not representations of actual cooked results.

Add a recipe using the ingredient editor (`name | quantity | unit | department`, one per line) and complete cooking steps. Tags, allergens, cost and nutrient estimates are editable. Users may manually transcribe recipes they have permission to use; automated URL extraction is not provided.

## Planning and leftovers

Select recipes and add them to the week. Use the 20-minute and budget shortcuts, or sort by time, estimated cost, protein, or best match. All selected meals are added in a single transaction. Your existing selections are preserved. Change weeks with the calendar arrows. The calendar starts compact: dinner slots and every already-planned meal remain visible. Turn on **Show empty breakfast, lunch & snack slots** when you want those extra spaces. Tap an empty slot to search and add a recipe directly, with the correct day already selected. Drag a meal to another day on desktop, or tap its title to choose the day on mobile. Edit one recipe without regenerating the rest. Mark cooked, skip, change portions, or lock a meal in the same editor.

To cook once for two meals, increase the original batch (for example, six portions), then reserve three portions as a linked leftover lunch. The original batch must retain at least one portion. Groceries buy six portions once. Hypothetical meal costs allocate the reserved portions to the leftover meal when it is marked cooked.

Approving records the week and generates groceries. Changes to approved weeks or locked meals require confirmation. History shows previous versions and can restore them. Copy previous week works only into an empty week, preserving existing selections. Print uses the browser's print dialog.

## Shopping and pantry

Pantry names should match canonical recipe names. Quantity deductions require compatible units and usable dates. Weight and volume are not interchanged without density rules; meat cuts are distinct. Update stock manually after cooking and after bringing groceries home. An old checkoff does not prove an item is currently in the pantry.

Recalculate groceries after inventory changes. Recipe sources remain traceable on each item. If quantities change after checking an item or entering its price, the revised record resets that old checkoff and total, retains the preceding information in notes/audit history, and asks you to review. Old offline edits to a replaced item are retained as conflicts.

Expand an item to enter the actual **whole-item amount paid**, store, quantity, shopper and note. Include that item's share of taxes and discounts in its paid amount. Choose **Finish shopping** to review all checked, unrecorded items and enter their actual prices together. A live total shows the single grocery expense you will record. Checkout is transactional: if another device changes a reviewed item, no partial prices or expense are saved. Previously recorded items are labeled and excluded from later checkout. Add real stock to Pantry separately. Do not also log that checkout as a second manual expense.

Search the shopping list by item, store or note. **Shopping mode** simplifies the page and enlarges its checkboxes; the finish-shopping bar stays within reach on mobile. Checking or unchecking an item offers Undo. Checked items move below the remaining items, or can be hidden altogether.

Shopping edits queue offline, survive reload, and synchronize when the host returns. A conflicted item pauses its own queued edits while other items continue synchronizing. Review conflicts explicitly. No financial transaction is inferred from a checkbox alone.

## Regular shopping items

Open **Groceries → Regular items** and save the usual weekly amount of milk, eggs, coffee, or household staples. Enable the items you want included. They join the list when a meal week is approved, or choose **Update this week’s list** even if you haven’t planned meals.

Regulars are minimum weekly requirements, not extras added on top of recipe needs. If recipes need 1 L of milk and your regular target is 1.5 L, the combined need is 1.5 L. If the pantry holds 250 ml, the list needs 1.25 L. Compatible units and aliases merge; materially different ingredients remain distinct. Manual grocery additions remain explicit extras. Unknown products or incompatible price units have no invented cost estimate; the shopping total is marked partial until actual prices are supplied. Pausing or deleting a regular affects the next list recalculation, rather than silently changing a shopping run in progress.

## Use what is already home

**Pantry → Ingredients** highlights recorded food with a reminder date within three days and lets you review past-date entries separately. The home screen surfaces these reminders when there is something to show.

**Pantry → Cook from pantry** compares actual recorded quantities against recipes scaled to household size. It prioritizes ingredients due soon, then the amount covered by stock. Each card shows missing quantities, including partially available ingredients. Expired stock, zero quantities, incompatible units and explicitly rejected recipes are excluded. Coverage describes recorded ingredients, not verified freshness or nutritional benefit. Suggestions are independent: the same pantry stock is not reserved for several recipes at once.

## Keep track of real leftovers

Open **Pantry → Leftovers** or choose **Save leftovers** from a recipe. Record what you actually put away, the number of saved portions, fridge/freezer location, cooking date, and an optional reminder date. No batch is invented just because a calendar meal was marked cooked.

Choose how many portions you used; every connected household member sees the remaining amount. Undo can restore a mistaken use. Discarding requires confirmation and tracks portions without inventing a financial loss. Finished batches can be included in the view and corrected by restoring a portion. Editing the original total preserves the number already used. Another member’s concurrent update requires review, and repeated requests cannot consume the same portion twice.

These ready-to-eat records are separate from planned leftover meals in the calendar. Recording or using a batch does not create another meal expense, approve a calendar meal, or automatically deduct raw pantry stock. Reminder dates are user-entered prompts, not a guarantee of safe storage. Source recipe allergens are captured when saving the batch; check actual ingredient labels and storage conditions.

## Cook without losing your place

Open a recipe, choose portions, then **Start cooking**. Check off gathered ingredients and move through the numbered steps one at a time. Your ingredient checklist and current step stay in the browser tab when you leave and return. The displayed ingredient amounts scale to the selected portions; instructions still refer to the recipe’s written method. Finishing the checklist does not automatically change inventory, spending, or meal completion.

Create up to six labeled kitchen timers while cooking. They use absolute deadlines, so background throttling or page reloads do not restart the countdown. Pause, resume, or dismiss them through the timer pill or cooking screen. Optional sound starts through a user action; browser restrictions can prevent sound, especially after reload. Visual finished indicators remain available. Timers are local to this device/tab and require HEARTH to remain open for alerts; they are not phone push notifications or an assurance that food is cooked safely. Signing out clears timers and local cooking progress.

## Prices and coupons

Record a real price with exact ingredient, product description, package amount/unit, store, source, observation validity, optional regular price, coupon amount **per package**, minimum package count, quality and eligibility. Only enter a coupon after checking its actual terms. Add exact loyalty/store/membership names you hold in Settings; ineligible offers are excluded.

JSON imports are reviewed user observations, not automatic retailer verification. No image OCR is provided. Historical observations remain visible but expired ones cannot win a comparison. Organic/grass-fed observations are excluded from the default standard-quality basket instead of silently substituting them. Price comparisons are limited to at most 20 candidate stores / three-stop combinations; travel is the explicit extra-mile assumption, not a computed driving route. Missing item prices mean a basket is partial, not a complete shopping quote.

The Kroger API is an unverified opt-in product-candidate connector requiring approved credentials and a location ID. It saves raw candidates to Host activity for review. Confirm package, quality, product compatibility, date and eligibility before entering one as a comparable observation. No other retailer API or live coupon feed is claimed. Follow official links for current ads.

## Spending and motivation

An expense total is base food + delivery/service fees + tip + tax − actual discount. All financial records use integer cents. Ingredient cost estimates do not enter actual expenses when you mark a meal cooked.

Remaining budget is the chosen budget minus recorded spending. Estimated takeout avoidance compares cooked portions against your explicitly configured per-person hypothetical baseline. It is not a cash deposit, does not reduce actual spending, and is not added to deal savings. Verified deal savings remain “Not established” without eligible purchase evidence and grounded comparable regular prices. Monthly/yearly forecasts are not implemented.

The Reality Check is optional, with Off, Gentle, Firm and Savage settings. It challenges a spending choice without shaming bodies or necessary meals. You can always log an order. Goals can track cooked meals, dollars remaining or manual progress. Link a reward to a goal and claim it after the milestone. No reward encourages undereating.

## Assistant and integrations

The default helper retrieves actual records and recipe candidates with deterministic rules. It is not described as an LLM. A message about "$55 takeout" creates a proposed transaction, not a recorded expense. Review its merchant, date, portions and components in a normal dollar-based form before confirmation. Technical JSON is available only under Advanced details; it is not needed for ordinary expense review. Completed proposals remain visibly completed after reload. Spending answers use readable amounts rather than raw cents or database fields.

Ollama and explicitly enabled OpenAI API providers can return structured operations against the real household tool registry. Invalid operations are rejected; interrupted Responses streams do not count as success. No model is downloaded automatically and no paid provider starts as an automatic fallback. Live inference is unverified in this cloud instance. ChatGPT subscription sign-in remains unavailable until its official protocol is verified and implemented.

## Reminders and privacy

Create opt-in schedules in Settings using your local date/time; timestamps are persisted as UTC instants. Planning reminders produce draft library ideas for review. They never approve meals or spend money. Tasks run only while the host is on. A failed provider task retries up to three times, then appears failed; remove and recreate its schedule after correcting access.

All three profiles share access. Sign out on shared devices to clear private browser cache; pending offline edits need review first. Provider credentials stay on the host. See BACKUP.md for export/restore and IPHONE.md for secure private access.
